### Featured Open-Source & Game Engineering Work

- **[`3d-cad-mcp-suite`](https://github.com/Melih-cell/3d-cad-mcp-suite)** — Open-source suite of **7 Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers** bridging **Maxon ZBrush**, **Autodesk Maya**, **Autodesk 3ds Max**, **Adobe Substance 3D Painter**, **Blender**, and **Autodesk AutoCAD**, complete with standalone `.OBJ` / `.DXF` pre-flight geometry & UV validation and automated High-to-Low PBR baking handoffs.
- **[Kızıl Katedral: Aksiyon RPG](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gotikgames.kizilkatedral)** — Dark gothic action RPG published on Google Play across 7 languages under Gotik Games / Veloce Labs.
- **ZombiCin (Unreal Engine 5)** — Folklore survival-horror PC/Console vertical slice featuring custom 25-bone creature rigging, High-to-Low PBR pipelines, and Lumen environments.
- **[Veloce Labs Mobile Portfolio](https://play.google.com/store/apps/developer?id=Veloce+Labs)** — 6 live commercial Android games & utilities (`Kızıl Katedral`, `Bilgi Arenası`, `Seri`, `SnapMind`, `LumiaWalls`, `Wallify`).
